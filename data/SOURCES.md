## Conversions
- 1 crore = 100 lakh = 10,000,000 rupees. FY26 lines are divided by 100, FY25 lines by 10,000,000.
- Cost of goods sold = purchases of stock-in-trade + changes in inventories.
- EBITDA = revenue from operations − cost of goods sold − employee benefit expense − other expenses (excludes other income). This reproduces the EBITDA DMart reports: Rs 5,255 crore in FY26.

## Not used
- FY24 line items: not available in the same machine-readable NSE format; the case compares the two latest audited years.
- Revenue per square foot as DMart reports it (on weighted-average area): the case uses its own simpler measure, revenue per square foot of year-end retail area, and labels it so.
